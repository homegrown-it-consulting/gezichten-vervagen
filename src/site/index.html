Vervaag de juiste gezichten op schoolfoto's, zonder dat er iets je computer verlaat.

Privacy

Wat de tool wél en níet doet

  • Detectie, geen herkenning. De tool weet wáár gezichten staan, niet wíe het is. Een mens beslist.
  • Je foto's verlaten je computer niet. Een ingebouwd beveiligingsbeleid verbiedt de pagina om gegevens naar welke server dan ook te sturen. Dat geldt ook voor de gedownloade versie.

  • Geen account, geen cookies, geen statistieken.

Free · offline · open about how it works

Blur the right faces in school photos, without anything leaving your computer.

After a school event you want to share photos, but not every child may be recognisable. This tool finds all faces; you click the ones to blur. No upload, no account, no face recognition.

Use it online

How it works

From a folder of photos to a clean set

Load

Drop a folder of photos into the tool (JPG, PNG or WebP).

Find

The tool finds where faces are, including small faces at the back of a group photo.

Choose

One overview shows every face. Click the ones to blur; draw in any face that was missed.

Export

You get new photos with an irreversible mosaic. Your originals stay untouched.

Privacy

What the tool does and doesn't do

  • Detection, not recognition. The tool knows where faces are, not who they are. A person decides.
  • Your photos never leave your computer. A built-in security policy forbids the page to send data to any server at all. That includes the downloaded copy.
  • Clean export. Metadata such as GPS location and camera details is removed from exported photos.
  • No account, no cookies, no analytics.
For your DPO / IT

Check it yourself

  • Open your browser's developer tools (F12), tab Network, and use the tool: after it has opened, nothing goes out. Only the installed app fetches updates of its own program files, never photos.
  • The security policy (connect-src 'none') is readable at the top of the HTML file.
  • Browser extensions fall outside that policy. For maximum certainty, use a browser profile without extensions.
  • The tool doesn't replace a consent policy: always check the result.

  • iPhone photos in HEIC format are not supported yet. Export them as JPG first.
